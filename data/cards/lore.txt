# CARD LORE — the Story tab in the card gallery.
#
# One block per card. A block starts with "===" and the card's id, and runs
# until the next one. Write whatever you like underneath; blank lines make
# paragraph breaks.
#
#   === grass-trailhead-novice
#   TITLE: Week One
#   She complained about the incline for six days.
#
#   "Then she bought the socks."
#
# TITLE: is optional — leave it out and the card's own name is used as the
# heading. A line on its own inside "double quotes" becomes the quote at the
# bottom of the panel; leave it out and the card's printed flavour text is used
# instead, which is already written for every card.
#
# Lines starting with # are notes to yourself and never appear in the game.
#
# NOTHING HERE CAN BREAK THE GAME. A card with no block, or an empty block,
# shows a short placeholder. A block for an id that does not exist is ignored
# and reported by "npm run lore". Delete anything, reorder anything, save.
#
# The id is what ties a block to a card, because three cards share a name. The
# card's name is written above each block so you can find it by eye.

=== boss-prisma-final-encore

# Cassia Cache — legendary leader
=== algo-leader-cassia-cache
TITLE: Tested Once, On Nothing
There is a delete function. She wrote the specification for it herself, in a
document nobody had asked for, and it works — she tested it in 2018 on an empty
file and watched the row go.

It has not been used since. Colleagues assume this is policy. It is a decision,
made every morning, by one person, about other people's worst nights.

# Don Sortino — legendary leader
=== algo-leader-don-sortino
TITLE: Every Meeting Was Requested
Nobody has ever been summoned. The diary is kept by an assistant who has twice
been asked by auditors whether that can really be true, and who has twice
produced the diary.

They arrive having already decided what they want. He has it. The espresso is
poured before they sit down, which is the only thing in the entire arrangement
he has ever initiated.

# Because You Watched — common action
=== algo-because-you-watched
TITLE: The Wording Was Chosen
"Because you watched" beat three other candidates in testing. "Recommended for
you" read as flattery. "You might like" read as a guess. The winner read as a

The copywriter has it on her CV as one line and has stopped explaining, at
parties, that it is the most-read sentence she will ever write.

# Loyalty Program — common action
=== algo-loyalty-program
TITLE: The Tier Above

reaching it moves your account onto a list that three people can see.

The list is not used for anything. It is checked by the woman who built it, who
wanted somewhere for the ones who stayed longest to go. She has never told any
of them and does not intend to.

# Preroll Runner — common character
=== algo-preroll-runner
TITLE: The First Five Seconds

including the man who wrote it.

He rehearsed all thirty. There is a turn in the middle where he lands a joke,

alive who know either exists are him and an editor who cut nothing.

"The bit about his mother is at twenty-two seconds."

# Re-Upload — common action
=== algo-re-upload

He could automate it in an afternoon and has not, and the reason is not
laziness. The day he does, the last human step in the process goes, and so does

# Deprecated Feature — common action

# Sorted For You — common action
=== algo-sorted-for-you
TITLE: Still In The Menu
Manual sort was never removed. It is three menus deep, it works perfectly, and
it is used by about one account in nine hundred, and the team responsible are
quietly proud of both halves of that sentence.

Removing it would generate a news cycle. Leaving it costs nothing and answers
the question before anybody asks it. Nobody planned that. It simply turned out
to be the cheapest kind of honesty.

# Mid-Roll Enforcer — rare character

# Shadow Archive — rare character

# Shadowban Notice — rare action

# Wi-Fi Outage — epic event
