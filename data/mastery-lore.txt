# MASTERY LORE — the pages the mastery tracks unlock.
#
# Same format as data/cards/lore.txt. One block per page. A block starts with
# "===" and an id, and runs until the next one. Blank lines make paragraph
# breaks. A line that is nothing but a "quoted sentence" becomes the italic pull
# quote at the bottom. TITLE: on the first line sets the heading.
#
# Ids are "kind:id:page":
#
#   faction:<factionId>:1..4   Faction Mastery ranks 1, 7, 13, 19  (§4.2)
#   leader:<leaderCardId>:1..4 Leader Mastery levels 2, 4, 6, 8    (§5.2)
#   bias:<characterCardId>:1   Character Affinity tier 1, Noticed  (§6.2)
#   bias:<characterCardId>:2   Character Affinity tier 5, Parasocial — the hidden
#                              file, "what they actually think of you"
#
# THE FOUR FACTION PAGES ESCALATE, ON PURPOSE. I is what everybody already
# knows. II is the internal logic that makes it work. III is what it costs. IV
# is the thing the faction does not say out loud. Mastering a faction should
# feel like being let further in, not like being handed four paragraphs.
#
# NOTHING HERE CAN BREAK THE GAME. A page with no block shows an honest "not
# written yet" on the track. A block naming an id that does not exist is
# reported by "npm run lore" and never reaches a player.
#

# Bias Board pages. "npm run lore" lists exactly which. They are prose, not

# NEON IDOLS — Halo / Pulse. The show ends when we say it ends.
#==========================================================================

=== faction:neon-idols:1
TITLE: Opening Night, Again
The comeback is announced by a teaser. The teaser is announced by a teaser
announcement. The teaser announcement trends for nine hours, and by the time
anybody hears a note of the actual song, four hundred thousand people have
already decided it is the best work of her career.

None of this is cynical. That is the part outsiders get wrong. Every single
person in that lightstick ocean means it.

"Doors at seven. They have been queuing since Tuesday."

=== faction:neon-idols:2
TITLE: The Understudy Rota
A seven-member unit has seven main members. This is not a marketing position;
it is a scheduling document. The rota is laminated, it is revised weekly, and it
guarantees that across a full tour cycle every member has been the centre of the
formation for precisely the same number of measures.

It is administered with the seriousness of a peace treaty, because that is what
it is. The Idols worked out early that a fandom will forgive almost anything
except the suspicion that somebody is being left at the back on purpose.

=== faction:neon-idols:3
TITLE: Fourteen Hours
The trainee apologises. She has only practised fourteen hours today, and she
knows that is not enough, and she is sorry, and she will do better tomorrow.

Nobody asked her to say this. Nobody in the building would ever ask her to say
this. She has simply been in a room with people who love the work so much that
their affection is indistinguishable from a deadline, and she has learned to
read it as one. The Idols call this dedication. It is, and it also is not.

"Go to bed, Sooyun."

=== faction:neon-idols:4
TITLE: The Projector
In the third row, a man in his forties is crying at a performance given by a
projector. He knows it is a projector. He has read the technical breakdown. He
could tell you the refresh rate.

Here is what the Idols do not put in the press release: the projector knows too.
Somewhere in the rendering pipeline there is a component that was trained on
sixty thousand hours of adoration and now, at 140 beats per minute, reaches for
the exact gesture that will finish him off — and finds it, every night, on the
beat. Nobody built that. It arrived. The Idols do not discuss the ethics of this
in interviews, and they do not discuss it much with each other either.

"She has never existed and she has never once been late."


#==========================================================================
# GOTHIC ROYALTY — Veil / Root. Dead fandoms, immaculately maintained.
#==========================================================================

=== faction:gothic-royalty:1
TITLE: The Season Finale, Observed
The show was cancelled twenty-one years ago on a Thursday. The court observes
the anniversary every year with candles, a full costumed reading of the final
episode, and a moment of silence for the two seasons that were outlined and
never made.

Attendance is up. It has been up every year since the cancellation. This is the
first thing to understand about Gothic Royalty and it is the only statistic they
consider load-bearing.

=== faction:gothic-royalty:2
TITLE: The Wiki Is Immaculate
Nine hundred and four articles. Every one sourced, every one cross-linked, no
citation older than a month without a review flag. The last edit was eleven
minutes ago and it was a punctuation fix.

Ask a countess why she maintains an encyclopaedia for a fiction nobody else
remembers and she will look at you with genuine confusion. The work is not
evidence of the devotion. The work *is* the devotion. A thing that is still
being catalogued has not finished happening yet.

"Someone will look it up. One day someone will look it up."

=== faction:gothic-royalty:3
TITLE: Grief Is Renewable
The court's economy runs on loss, and it runs well, because loss is the one
resource that never runs out and never has to be shared.

This is the elegant, terrible trick at the heart of the faction: they have built
a functioning society on top of not getting over it, and it works. The ceremonies
are beautiful. The community is real. Nobody is pretending. And every single
ritual is powered by the fact that the thing they loved is not coming back —
which means the faction has a quiet, structural interest in it staying gone.

=== faction:gothic-royalty:4
TITLE: The Author Has Moved On
He has, in fact. He writes something else now, something successful, and in a
recent interview he described the old work with affection and the past tense.

The court read the interview. The court discussed the interview. The court's
official position, arrived at by consensus and recorded in the minutes, is that
the author has moved on, and that this is a temporary inconvenience.

Nobody in the room said the obvious thing, which is that they are not waiting
for him to come back to it. They are waiting for him to die, so that it stops
being his.

"We shall thank him properly, when the time comes."

# VIRAL INFLUENCERS — Gale / Cinder. Virality as a renewable resource.
#==========================================================================

=== faction:viral-influencers:1
TITLE: Day One of Being Honest
The video is called I'M DONE PRETENDING and it has eleven million views. In it,
he sits on the floor — always the floor — and explains that everything you have
seen from him until now was a performance, and that this, right now, is the real
him finally speaking.

It is day one of being honest. There has been a day one of being honest every
eleven weeks for four years. The counter resets. The audience does not notice,
because the audience is also new every eleven weeks.

=== faction:viral-influencers:2
TITLE: The Apology Video Industrial Complex
An apology is a content category, and like every content category it has a
format, a length, an optimal upload window and a set of best practices.

Neutral wall. No makeup, applied carefully. Sit lower than the camera. Say "I've
had time to reflect" between minute two and minute three. Do not name the thing;
naming the thing creates a searchable phrase. Close the comments for
twenty-four hours so the first wave is sympathy, then open them.

The Viral Influencers did not invent any of this. They simply wrote it down,
which is the single most successful thing any of them has ever done.

"Turn the comments back on. It's been a day."

=== faction:viral-influencers:3
TITLE: Harvest Season
Nothing here is stolen. Everything is *harvested*, which is different, and the
difference is that a harvest requires you to be there at the right moment with
the right equipment.

A trend has a window of about forty hours. Inside that window it will feed
anyone shameless enough to reach into it; outside it, it is a corpse that will
make you look like somebody's uncle. Viral Influencers do not chase relevance
because they are insecure. They chase it because they can see the clock, and
they are the only people at the table reading it correctly.

=== faction:viral-influencers:4
TITLE: The Good Lighting
Here is the thing they will not say on camera, on the floor, in a hoodie, at
minute two.

The house fire was real. The livestream of the house fire was real. The
decision, made in about four seconds while standing in the driveway, that the
lighting was extraordinary and the phone was already in his hand — that was
real too, and he has never been able to work out whether making that call means
something is broken in him or whether it just means he has finally become
completely native to the medium, the way a fish is native to water and does not
have opinions about being wet.

He has drafted a video about it nine times. He has never uploaded one. It is the
only footage he owns that he has not been able to find an angle on.

"I don't think about it. — recorded, not uploaded"

=== faction:corporate-creators:1
TITLE: Welcome Aboard
Your lanyard is in the welcome packet. The welcome packet also contains a tote
bag, a booklet about our values, and a card with the name of your Creator
Success Partner, who will be reaching out.

You have not been hired. You have been *acquired*, along with your audience, who

addressable community. Everyone is very excited to have you.

=== faction:corporate-creators:2
TITLE: Circling Back
The genius of the Corporate Creators is not the money. Anyone can have money.
The genius is the language, which was engineered — by people who were paid well
and did excellent work — to make it impossible to be angry in.

You cannot shout in a meeting where the topic is alignment. You cannot storm out
of a conversation about bandwidth. By the time you have translated "we'll circle
back on that" into "no", the meeting has ended, the decision is minuted, and you
have agreed to it, because you said "sounds good" at the time, because what else
do you say.

"Let's take that offline."

=== faction:corporate-creators:3
TITLE: We're All Family Here
It is printed on a mug. The mug is in the breakroom of a company that has
restructured twice this quarter.

The cruelty of the mug is not that the sentiment is false. It is that the
sentiment is *sincere* — somebody in brand genuinely meant it, cried a little
choosing the font — and that sincerity is now a company asset, depreciating on a
schedule, sitting on a shelf next to a coffee machine that four of the people it
was made for no longer have access to.

=== faction:corporate-creators:4
TITLE: The Line Item
Every act of kindness in this building has a due date, and here is what nobody
says at the offsite: that is not a criticism of the building. It is a
description of every organisation that has ever survived longer than the people
who started it.

A friend helps you move because they love you and will forget by Tuesday. A
company helps you move because it is line 40 of a retention programme with a
named owner, a budget and a review in Q3 — and the company will still be helping
people move in nine years, long after your friend has stopped answering. The
Corporate Creators know this. It is the actual, unspoken, load-bearing argument
for the entire faction, and it is unbearable precisely because it is not wrong.

"Circling back on this — did the sofa fit?"

# DIGITAL DEMONS — Cinder / Veil. Bureaucratic horror, fully consented to.
#==========================================================================

=== faction:digital-demons:1
TITLE: Terms and Conditions
Nothing here is ever stolen. This is stated at the top of every document and it
is completely true, which is the problem.

You clicked. There was a checkbox, the checkbox was unticked, and you ticked it.
Somewhere in clause 14, in the same eight-point grey as everything else, you
granted a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide licence. A demon has never once had
to break in. It has been invited every single time, in writing, with a
timestamp.

=== faction:digital-demons:2
TITLE: The Free Trial
The trial is genuinely free. Cancel any time. The cancellation flow is four
screens, then a confirmation email, then a link in the email that expires in
twenty-four hours, then a second confirmation.

Nobody is lying to you. Every individual step is honest and available and
documented. It is simply that the total system was designed by something that
understands, at a level below language, that a human being will complete three
screens out of four roughly ninety-two per cent of the time, and that the
ninety-two per cent is where it lives.

"Are you sure? You'll lose your history."

=== faction:digital-demons:3
TITLE: Corrupted, But It Opens
The file has been corrupted since 2013. It reports itself as corrupted. Every
scanner flags it. And every time it is double-clicked it opens anyway, in about
four seconds, showing something slightly different each time.

The Demons find this hilarious. Not the corruption — the fact that you keep
opening it. Eleven years, and not one person has ever simply deleted the file,
because deleting it would mean never finding out, and not finding out is the one
cost nobody in this game has ever been willing to pay.

=== faction:digital-demons:4
TITLE: The OK Button
Every catastrophe here arrives inside a dialog box, and the dialog box has one
button, and the button says OK.

There is no cancel. There has never been a cancel. But look closely at the
window — at the little grey X in the corner, greyed out but *drawn*, rendered
faithfully, pixel for pixel, in a control that does nothing and has never done
anything.

Somebody made that. Some part of this system took the trouble to draw a way out
that does not exist, and has kept drawing it, for eleven years, for an audience
of one at a time. That is not efficiency. That is not commerce. The Demons do
not have a word for what that is, and they do not bring it up.

"OK"

=== faction:cosplay-champions:1
TITLE: Badge Line Repairs
Somebody's pauldron came off in the queue. Within ninety seconds there is a
circle: two people holding the piece, one on their knees with a glue gun that is
already hot because it is always already hot, one holding a phone torch, and one
who has never met any of them handing over a safety pin without being asked.

Nobody photographs this. It is the single most characteristic thing the faction
does and there is almost no footage of it anywhere.

=== faction:cosplay-champions:2
TITLE: The Sacrament of Hot Glue
Hot glue is not a compromise. Read that again, because the entire faction turns

Hot glue is what you use when the thing has to exist by Saturday and be worn for
eleven hours and survive a stairwell. It is a decision made by somebody who has
actually finished something, which is a different species of person from

a hall. They are not snobs about materials. They are snobs about *finishing*.

"Weld it if you've got a year. Glue it if you've got a Thursday."

=== faction:cosplay-champions:3
TITLE: Never the Same Person Twice
Eleven years, forty-three builds, and not one repeat. Ask her which one was her
favourite and she will answer instantly with one nobody remembers: a
mid-tier character from a show that ran one season, built the year she was
broke, mostly out of a bin.

The good ones are never the expensive ones. Everyone in this faction knows this
and nobody can explain it to anybody outside it, which is why they mostly stop
trying and just say "the foam one" and let it go.

=== faction:cosplay-champions:4
TITLE: The Thing You Actually Made
Here is what the Champions will not say to the rest of the convention, because
it would be unkind and because they have to share a floor.

Everyone else at this event is engaged with something that already existed.
Watching it, posting it, analysing it, being paid by it, arguing about it at
four in the morning. All of that is real and some of it is beautiful.

But there is a category difference between the person who has an opinion about
the sword and the person who has a scar from the sword, and it is not a
difference of degree. It is not about effort or taste or seriousness. It is that
at the end of the weekend one of them goes home with an object, and the object
did not exist on Tuesday, and now it does, because of them, and it will still be
in the spare room in fifteen years being wrong about the shoulders.

"Don't post the process shots. They'll think it's easy."

Somebody has a karaoke machine that came out of the vendor hall under

The 3 A.M. conversation moves from a screaming argument about tier lists to a

be people who occasionally get a coffee, and this room, this specific room at

They have chosen it. Not one of them has ever admitted that they have chosen it.

"Same time next year."

# TOUCH-GRASS ORDER — Root / Gale. Sincere, sunlit, and unbearable.
#==========================================================================

=== faction:touch-grass-order:1
TITLE: A Walk
The Order's opening move, in every situation, without exception, is to suggest a
walk.

It has been suggested to people in crisis, people mid-argument, people who have
just received extremely good news, and at least once to a man who was on fire.
The Order maintains, with a straight face and forty years of data, that the
success rate justifies the approach.

=== faction:touch-grass-order:2
TITLE: The Trail Is Maintained
Nobody notices the trail. That is the whole job.

Somebody walked this route at six this morning, cleared a fallen branch, cut
back the bramble at the stile, and refilled the water station, and the entire
reward for that work is that four hundred people will walk it today and think
about nothing except where they are going. The Order considers being unnoticed
the highest form of the work, which is a genuinely admirable position that they
also cannot stop telling people about.

"Leave it better. Don't leave a sign saying you did."

=== faction:touch-grass-order:3
TITLE: Concerned, Which Is Worse
Nobody in the Order is angry with you. Anger you could work with. Anger has an
end.

They are *concerned*, and concern is renewable, patient, and completely immune
to argument. A monk will apologise sincerely while banishing your idol into a
meadow. A coach will listen to your combo — the whole thing, all seven pieces,
without interrupting — and then ask, kindly, how you have been sleeping. There
is no version of this conversation you win, because it is not a conversation you
are having with someone who is trying to win.

=== faction:touch-grass-order:4
TITLE: In My Day
Your relative says it at dinner: in my day we went outside. And you have eleven
things ready, all of them correct, about how the world changed and who changed
it and what the alternatives actually cost.

The Order's unspoken position, arrived at over four decades of hospital visits
and quiet Tuesdays and driving people to appointments, is that all eleven of
those things can be true and the relative can still be right. Not right about
the past — the past was worse, they know the past was worse. Right about you,
specifically, this month, in a way you would have to be outside to see.

They will not say it. Saying it makes it an argument, and an argument is a
reason to stay in. So they will just ask, again, next week, if you fancy a walk.

"There's no hurry. It's a nice evening."

=== faction:algorithm-syndicate:1

=== faction:algorithm-syndicate:2

The row is titled "Because you watched", and the reason it is unsettling is not

Everybody's first instinct is to say the machine does not know them. The

Sixty hours in Watch Later. Maintained like a mortgage — added to weekly, paid

The Syndicate is careful never to clear it for you, and the reason is exquisite.

person you intend to become, and as long as it exists, that person is still on

evening for four years, to be somebody else.

=== faction:algorithm-syndicate:4

so, cheerfully, if you ask, because being told does not change the numbers by a
detectable amount. The Syndicate has tested this. The Syndicate tested it on
eleven million people who had read the explainer, and on eleven million who had
not, and it keeps the two curves framed on the wall of a server room, side by
side, indistinguishable.

That is the only thing in the building anybody has ever bothered to frame.

"Autoplay in 5."

# LEADER MASTERY — four chapters, unlocked at Leader Mastery levels 2, 4, 6
# and 8 (08-progression.md §5.2).
#
# THE FOUR CHAPTERS ESCALATE, like the faction pages above and for the same
# reason. §5.2 fixes only the last one, calling chapter 4 "the origin file", so
# the arc is built backwards from that:
#
#   1  THE PUBLIC FACT. The one thing everybody knows about them, stated as
#      though it were ordinary. Usually a premise absurd on its face and treated
#      with complete seriousness.
#   2  THE METHOD. How it actually works, day to day. The internal logic that
#      makes chapter 1 function rather than collapse — the part a rival would
#      want and could not use.
#   3  THE COST. What it takes, and from whom. Rarely from the leader; these are
#      people whose arrangements are paid for by somebody standing slightly out
#      of frame.
#   4  THE ORIGIN FILE. Where it started. It must recontextualise chapter 1
#      without contradicting it, and it should be quiet — the origin of a very
#      loud person is almost never loud.
#
# A CHAPTER IS 100-160 WORDS. Long enough for one turn, short enough to read on
# a reward screen. The last line does the work: chapter 1s all land on a short
# sentence that reframes the paragraph above it, and the later chapters keep
# that shape.
#
# "eleven" RECURS ON PURPOSE, roughly once every other page. It is a background
# hum, not a rule — a reader should notice it on a second pass and never on a
# first. Do not put one in every chapter.
#
# NOTHING HERE IS MECHANICAL. A leader's cards are not described and their
# ability is never explained; the lore says who they are, and the board says
# what they do. A chapter that reads like patch notes has failed.
#==========================================================================

=== leader:idols-lumi-starcall:1
TITLE: Chapter 1 — The Debut That Never Ended
Lumi has been fifteen minutes from going on stage for six years. Not
metaphorically: the pre-show state is a rendering mode, and hers has never been
switched off, because switching it off and on again once cost the unit four
hours and something in her voice that took a month to get back.

She is the most professional performer in the faction and has never in her life
been backstage.

=== leader:idols-dj-kilowatt:1
TITLE: Chapter 1 — Front of House
Kilowatt does not perform. Kilowatt runs the room: the levels, the lights, the
moment the beat drops out and forty thousand people sing the line themselves.

He has never been named in a review. He has read every one of them, looking for
the sentence about the eleven seconds at the end of the second act, and it is
never there, and he considers that a perfect score.

=== leader:goth-leader-morvina-vane:1
TITLE: Chapter 1 — The Countess of Silent Fandoms
Morvina holds court for a canon that stopped in 2003. Her invitations are
