TITLE: The Server Is Closing
FACTION: Gothic Royalty
ABOUT: The Manor has been running for sixteen years for a canon that ended in 2009. Its host has posted a shutdown date. Morvina treats the announcement as a scheduling error, the court treats it as a funeral, and the scavengers treat it as inventory.


=== EPISODE: The Announcement Post

NARRATION: THE MANOR — a self-hosted persistent world, four percent occupancy, sixteen years uptime. Nobody has patched it since the year the source material ended.

POST: SERVICE NOTICE. This host will be decommissioned in thirty (30) days. Please export any data you wish to retain. Thank you for sixteen years.

Countess Morvina Vane: Warden. There is a notice on the gate.
Warden Elowe: There is, my lady.
Countess Morvina Vane: It has a date on it.
Warden Elowe: It does.
Countess Morvina Vane (composed): Then it is a scheduling matter. Schedules are moved. Have it moved.

NARRATION: Warden Elowe has moderated an empty forum for six years. She has never once asked to be relieved.

Warden Elowe: I have written to them. Twice.
Countess Morvina Vane: And?
Warden Elowe: The second reply was the same as the first, with a different name at the bottom.

NARRATION: Something arrives at the east door. It is not a guest. It is carrying a manifest.

The Migration Daemon (flat): ARCHIVE PASS ONE OF FOUR. ENUMERATING GEOMETRY. ENUMERATING FURNITURE. ENUMERATING PERSONS.
Countess Morvina Vane: I beg your pardon.
The Migration Daemon (flat): PERSONS ARE FURNITURE FOR PACKING PURPOSES. THIS IS NOT A JUDGEMENT. IT IS A SCHEMA.

=== EPISODE: Court of the Silent Fandom

NARRATION: The Long Gallery. Four chairs, three occupied, and above the fireplace a portrait of somebody who logged off in 2014 and never came back.

Alaric Thornheart (drawling): I move that we do nothing. It has served us admirably for sixteen years.
Countess Morvina Vane: You move that we do nothing about everything, Alaric. It is your only motion.
Alaric Thornheart: It is a good motion. Look how well the Manor has done under it.

  Warden Elowe: The daemon was courteous, for what it is worth. It said thank you at the gate.
  Alaric Thornheart: Of course it did. They always do. That is the part that gets you.
OTHERWISE:
  Warden Elowe: We put it out of the window, my lady. It came back through the window.
  Alaric Thornheart: Then it has learned the house better than half the court has.

NARRATION: There are three positions, and the court has taken all of them at once: preserve it, port it, or let it perish beautifully.

Alaric Thornheart: I have been writing my memoirs for eleven years. I am up to the second week.
Countess Morvina Vane: Alaric.
Alaric Thornheart (sharper): My point, Regent, is that a thing that is finished is *finished*, and we are the only people in the world who have not noticed. I would like to be allowed to not finish something in peace.
Countess Morvina Vane: You would like the ending to be your idea.
Alaric Thornheart: I would like the ending to be *somebody's* idea. It is currently a billing decision.

NARRATION: He is not wrong, and being not wrong has never once stopped a duel in this house.

Alaric Thornheart (rising): Regent's Prerogative, then. Take it from me at the table and the court is yours to point at whatever you like.

BATTLE: Alaric Thornheart
  PLAYS: Alaric Thornheart
  DIFFICULTY: casual
  RULE: the court reconvenes
  IF YOU LOSE:
    Alaric Thornheart (bowing): Again, then. It is not as though either of us has an appointment.

Alaric Thornheart: Well. Yes. That is why you are the Regent and I am the man with eleven years of second week.
Countess Morvina Vane: You will help.
Alaric Thornheart: I will help. I will complain the entire time and I will help.
Warden Elowe: I have made a list of everything that is only in here.
Countess Morvina Vane: How long is it?
Warden Elowe: Four hundred and six items, my lady. I did not include the people.


=== EPISODE: The Archive Rats

NARRATION: Day nineteen. The Manor's west wing has been getting lighter. Not brighter — lighter. Things are going.

Warden Elowe: Scrapers. Three of them, in the undercroft, with a cart and a very good ladder.
Alaric Thornheart: A cart. In my undercroft.
Warden Elowe: It is not your undercroft, my lord, it is the Manor's.
Alaric Thornheart: That was going to be my next point and you have taken it.

NARRATION: They are not vandals. They are meticulous, they are insured, and they have done this to eleven worlds this year.

Countess Morvina Vane: You are looting a house with the family still in it.
The Scraper Crew: We are photographing a house that is on fire. You are welcome to keep standing in it.

=== EPISODE: Last Login

NARRATION: The fourteenth. Four percent occupancy is now, briefly, forty. Everybody who ever had an account has logged in for the last night, and the Manor has not been this loud since the canon ended.

Juno Pale: There are so many people. Where were they all?
Warden Elowe: Here, miss. Just not at the same time.

IF took the mirror:
  NARRATION: The mirror goes live at eight. The same rooms, the same wallpaper, a different owner, and a gate with no date on it.
  Alaric Thornheart: It is identical.
  Countess Morvina Vane: It is identical.
  Alaric Thornheart: I hate it enormously and I am going to log in every day.
OTHERWISE:
  NARRATION: There is no mirror. At midnight the host pulls the disk and four hundred and six things stop existing, and everybody in the Long Gallery knows the exact minute.
  Alaric Thornheart: I have finished the memoirs.
  Countess Morvina Vane: You have not.
  Alaric Thornheart: No. But I have written the last line, and I am going to read it out at eleven, and nobody is going to be able to check the middle.

NARRATION: At half past ten, the candles in the Long Gallery go out in order, from the door inward, which is not a thing the Manor has ever done.

The Widow (softly): You are all still here. Good. Nobody go anywhere.

NARRATION: She is not from the Manor. She has been keeping a vigil somewhere with worse wallpaper, and she has read the shutdown notice, and she has come to make sure this one never finishes either.

The Widow (softly): I have held nine of these. Nine worlds, nine last nights, and not one of them has ever been allowed to close. Sit down. It does not have to be over. It never has to be over.
Countess Morvina Vane: ...Warden. Who is that.
Warden Elowe (quietly): That is what I would have been, my lady. In another six years.

BATTLE: The Widow of Dead Fandoms
  PLAYS: The Widow of Dead Fandoms
  DIFFICULTY: boss
  RULE: the court reconvenes
  IF YOU LOSE:

    Countess Morvina Vane: I know. I have been you since 2009. Again.

NARRATION: The Widow does not leave so much as stop insisting. The candles come back up, all at once, and the Long Gallery is extremely full and extremely bright and has eighty minutes left.

Countess Morvina Vane: Warden Elowe.
Warden Elowe: My lady.
Countess Morvina Vane: You have moderated an empty room for six years, and I have never once thanked you for it, because thanking you would have meant admitting the room was empty.
Warden Elowe: I know, my lady.
Countess Morvina Vane: Thank you.
