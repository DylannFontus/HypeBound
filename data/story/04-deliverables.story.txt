TITLE: Deliverables
FACTION: Corporate Creators
ABOUT: Tobi Renn signs the best contract of his life and disappears into it. A year in six episodes: the calendar, the notes, the streak, the day nothing goes out, and the renegotiation. Burnout written as an invoice rather than a character flaw.


=== EPISODE: Q1 Content Calendar

NARRATION: A studio floor with a snack wall, four edit bays and a light above the door that has not been off since it was installed.

Cressida Vale: Tobi Renn. Sit anywhere. The chairs are all the same chair.
Tobi Renn (grinning): This is — sorry, is that a whole wall of snacks?
Cressida Vale: It is a whole wall of snacks and it is the single most effective retention tool this company has ever purchased. It cost less than one edit bay.

NARRATION: He is twenty-six, he shoots on a phone in a flat with bad curtains, and he has just been offered five years of not worrying about money.

Cressida Vale: Here is what I am not going to do. I am not going to tell you it will not change the work. It will change the work.
Tobi Renn: Okay.
Cressida Vale: I am also not going to tell you the change is bad. Most people who sign this get better. You will get better. You will get better at a rate that will frighten you.

IF signed the deal:
  Tobi Renn: You did the idol thing, right? The reunion? My sister had tickets.
  Cressida Vale: We funded the reunion, yes.
  Tobi Renn: Did they mind? Giving you the name?
  Cressida Vale (evenly): They minded. They signed, and they minded, and both of those are still true, and I would do it again because twelve thousand people saw a show that nine hundred were going to see.
  Tobi Renn: ...That's a really honest answer.
  Cressida Vale: I have found that honest answers cost me almost nothing and buy me four years.
OTHERWISE:
  Tobi Renn: You did the idol thing, right? Did they sign?
  Cressida Vale: They did not.
  Tobi Renn: Ha! Good for them.
  Cressida Vale: Yes. It was. They played to nine hundred people and it was the best night of their lives and I have thought about it more than I would like to.

NARRATION: The calendar goes up on the wall. It is beautiful. It is a year, in colour, and every square has something in it.

Tobi Renn: That's — how many is that?
Cressida Vale: One hundred and fifty-six.
Tobi Renn: In a year?
Cressida Vale: Three a week. It is the number the format needs. It is not negotiable, it is not aspirational, and it is the reason the money is what it is.
Tobi Renn (delighted): I can do three a week. I've BEEN doing three a week, in a flat, for free.

NARRATION: He has been doing three a week in a flat for free. That is exactly why they signed him, and exactly why nobody in the room says the next part out loud.

Cressida Vale: Then we start Monday. There is a channel doing our format in our slot with a bigger budget, and by Friday there will not be.

=== EPISODE: The Optimization Meeting

NARRATION: Week nine. Forty-one videos. The numbers are extremely good and the meeting is on the calendar anyway, because the meeting is always on the calendar.

The Optimization Note (flat): FIRST EIGHT SECONDS. THE SUBJECT IS INTRODUCED AT ELEVEN. MOVE THE SUBJECT TO TWO.
Tobi Renn: The eleven seconds is the joke. The joke is that it takes a while.
The Optimization Note (flat): RETENTION AT ELEVEN SECONDS IS SIXTY-ONE PERCENT. RETENTION AT TWO SECONDS IS EIGHTY-FOUR PERCENT. THE JOKE IS TWENTY-THREE PERCENT.

NARRATION: Nobody in the room disagrees with it, because it is not an opinion. It is a measurement, and it is correct.

Tobi Renn: Cressida. Back me up.
Cressida Vale: I am going to back you up and you are not going to like how.
Cressida Vale: The note is right. Move the subject to two. And then use the eleven seconds you just saved on something else you want, and do not tell anybody what it is for, and in six weeks when it works it becomes the format and the note will recommend it back to you as if it thought of it.
Tobi Renn: That's — that's horrible?
Cressida Vale: That is the job. You are being told what performs. You are not being told what to make. People who cannot tell those apart last about fourteen months.

BATTLE: The Optimization Note
  PLAYS: Don Sortino
  DIFFICULTY: casual
  RULE: sponsor segment
  IF YOU LOSE:
    The Optimization Note (flat): NOTE ISSUED. NOTE UNRESOLVED. RE-ISSUING.
    Cressida Vale: Again. It does not get bored and neither do we.

Tobi Renn: Does it ever stop?
Cressida Vale: The notes? No.
Tobi Renn: Right.
Cressida Vale: Tobi. The notes are not the thing to watch. Watch the calendar.
