# Chapter 6 of the HYPEBOUND story campaign.
#
# The design doc (§3.7) gives this chapter a Side Cut, "The Stairwell Workshop",
# unlocked by Branch A. It was written as a branch-varied scene inside Episode 4
# because the format had no optional episode; the format has one now, and the
# Stairwell Workshop has been lifted back out into the episode it was always
# meant to be. Episode 4 keeps its own visit to that landing, seventeen hours
# later and an hour before the walk — Vera is on it all weekend, which is the
# point of her.
#
# Reads two decisions from earlier chapters (Ratio, Render Unto). Both are
# bonuses — every one has an OTHERWISE, and the chapter reads complete for a
# player who has never touched another one.

TITLE: Best in Show
FACTION: Cosplay Champions
ABOUT: Three days, one hall, one judging table. Vera Foamhammer has won everything worth winning and now spends her cons fixing other people's costumes on a stairwell landing. Sunny Patchwell has built something extraordinary and has no idea.


=== EPISODE: Hall Costume

NARRATION: HALL C — Thursday, 14:20. Twenty-two hours to prejudging. The carpet is a crime and everybody has agreed not to discuss it.

NARRATION: Vera Foamhammer carries two crates and a folding table past the freight door, down one flight, and sets up on the landing between C and D, which is where she has worked every con for four years.

Vera Foamhammer: Table. Lamp. Hallway repair kit — needle, magnets, spare thermoplastic. The sacred adhesive.
Vera Foamhammer: Right. Open for business, and it is Thursday, and it will be somebody within the hour.

NARRATION: There is a pitch by the freight door with four hundred enamel pins on it and a laminated sign, and neither the pins nor the sign are the man's own work.

Denny Reprint (cheerful): Morning. Four quid each, three for a tenner.
Vera Foamhammer: Whose are they?
Denny Reprint: Not mine.
Vera Foamhammer: I know they're not yours, Denny, I'm asking whose.
Denny Reprint: Also not mine, that's the honest answer. Four hundred pins, four quid each, and not one of them is mine. I've never once said otherwise, and you're the first person all weekend who's asked.

NARRATION: Somewhere in the badge line a first-time entrant is holding a left wing across both arms, because the shoulder mount has been worrying her since Tuesday and she does not yet know anybody to ask.

Sunny Patchwell: Sorry — sorry, is the — do you know if there's somewhere I can put this down?
Vera Foamhammer: Down one flight. There's a table.
Sunny Patchwell: Oh, brilliant, thank you — I don't want to take up your —
Vera Foamhammer: It's a table for putting things down on. That is its entire function. Go.

NARRATION: The wing goes on the table. Vera looks at it for slightly too long, the way she looks at things she has not worked out yet.

Vera Foamhammer: Who made this?
Sunny Patchwell (apologetic): Me. Sorry. Is it —
Vera Foamhammer: I did not say anything was wrong with it.

BATTLE: Denny Reprint
  PLAYS: Skree Nine-Tabs
  YOU PLAY: Vera Foamhammer
  DIFFICULTY: beginner
  RULE: clip farm
  IF YOU LOSE:
    Denny Reprint: Volume, that's all it is. I move three hundred a con and I don't need any of them to be good.
    Vera Foamhammer: Again.

Denny Reprint (packing): Fine. I'll move the table four feet and we'll say the freight door was always yours.
Vera Foamhammer: The freight door was always mine.
Denny Reprint: It was, yeah. You've had it since before I started and I've never pretended otherwise.

NARRATION: The pins stay. Vera has never once asked anybody to leave a con, and Denny has never once lied about what he sells, and those two facts have coexisted at this event for four years.

Vera Foamhammer (to Sunny): Shoulder strut's going to go.
Sunny Patchwell: It's fine, it's held since Tuesday —
Vera Foamhammer: I'm not telling you it's bad work. I am telling you it is going to go, because the crush by the escalator does not care how good the work is. Bring it here when it does.


=== EPISODE: Repair Sortie

NARRATION: Friday, 11:41. Prejudging opens at 12:21.

NARRATION: The left wing shears at the shoulder strut in the crush by the escalator, exactly where it was going to, and Sunny Patchwell sits down on the floor of the fire lane holding two pieces of something she spent seven months on.

  Sunny Patchwell: I'm in Novice.
  Vera Foamhammer: You are.
