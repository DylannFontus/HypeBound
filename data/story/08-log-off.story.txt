TITLE: Log Off
FACTION: Touch-Grass Order
ABOUT: Ivo Marsh logged off for two years on the Order's own programme, came back healthy, and found that every friendship he had was a posting schedule that kept running without him. Three dated days at a retreat, a plastic tub, a sign-out log, and a room forty miles away that he is not in.
