TITLE: Repost
FACTION: Meme Collective
ABOUT: Two communities go to war over who posted a picture of a frog in a hat first. Nine years of evidence, a laminated binder, sixty-one reaction images and zero jurisdiction. Then somebody points the joke at the man who made it, and the Old Board finds out that a bylaw written as a joke in year two is the only thing holding the door.


=== EPISODE: The Original Post

NARRATION: THE OLD BOARD. Nine years old, one moderator, four hundred people who have never met and a binder that is laminated because of an incident.

POST: THE HAT — a photograph of a frog wearing a small hat. Posted 03:12 on a Tuesday, nine years ago, by GROUT. Body text: none. Title: none. Known reposts: 41,206 and counting, logged by the Vault across every site that has since closed.

Chairperson Nobody: The Chair recognises that a claim has been made.
Skree Nine-Tabs: They're saying it's theirs.
Chairperson Nobody: The Chair recognises that a claim has been made, Skree, and the Chair would like the minutes to reflect that it was recognised calmly.

NARRATION: There is no Chair. There is a folding chair, and whoever sits in it is the Chair, and this has been true for nine years and has never once produced a problem, which is itself the most frightening fact about the Old Board.

Vint: Our archive has it four days earlier. I'm not being funny —
Skree Nine-Tabs: You are being extremely funny. That's the whole thing you do.
Vint: I'm not being funny, I'm being accurate. Four days. It's dated.

Grout: I posted it at ten past three in the morning because I couldn't sleep and there was a frog on the bins.
Vint: There is no frog on any bins in your photo.
Grout: The frog was ON the bins. Then I put a hat on it. In an image editor. Obviously.
Vint: See, that's the bit where I stop being able to follow you.

NARRATION: This is what the war is. It will run for eleven days and produce more written material than the founding of most nations.
