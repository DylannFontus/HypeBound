#     (for example: 02-the-server-is-closing.story.txt)
#  2. Delete these notes and write your own story.
#  3. Save. That's it — the game finds it on its own.
#
#  Files are listed in the game in file-name order, so numbering them at the
#  front (01-, 02-, 03-) is how you decide what comes first.
#
#  Any line starting with # is a note to yourself. The game never shows it.
#  Blank lines are free — use as many as you like.

# ── The chapter's details. These three lines go at the very top. ─────────────

TITLE: The Name Of Your Chapter
FACTION: Neon Idols
ABOUT: One or two sentences describing the chapter. This is what the player reads on the chapter list before they start.


# ── Episode one. A chapter can have as many episodes as you like. ────────────

=== EPISODE: The Name Of Your First Episode

# Narration is anything the reader sees that nobody says out loud.
NARRATION: A room, somewhere, at an unreasonable hour.

# A character speaks by putting their name before a colon. That's the whole
# rule — you never have to introduce a character or list them anywhere.
Lumi Starcall: This is how somebody says something.
Rin Halfstep: And this is somebody else answering.

# Brackets after the name set their expression. Any word works.
Lumi Starcall (smiling): You can put a mood in brackets.
Rin Halfstep (nervous): Any word at all. It shows next to their name.

# POST puts something on screen as a social-media post instead of speech.
POST: 4,180 people are watching this. Comments are disabled.


# ── A choice. Everything under an option only happens if it's picked. ────────

CHOICE: What do you want to ask the player?
  * The first thing they can pick.
      REMEMBER: they were kind
      Lumi Starcall: This line only plays if they picked the first one.
  * The second thing they can pick.
      Lumi Starcall: And this one only plays if they picked the second.

# Back out at this indentation, so this plays whichever they picked.
Rin Halfstep: Anyway.


# ── Reacting to a choice, here or in a much later episode. ───────────────────

IF they were kind:
  Lumi Starcall: You remembered.
OTHERWISE:
  Lumi Starcall: You didn't, and that's fine too.


# ── A battle. Only the first line is required. ───────────────────────────────

BATTLE: The Name Of Whoever They Fight
  PLAYS: Cyra Swipe
  DIFFICULTY: casual
  IF YOU LOSE:
    Lumi Starcall: These lines play after a loss, then the battle is offered again.

Lumi Starcall: And this plays once they've won.
