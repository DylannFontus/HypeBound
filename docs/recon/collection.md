# Recon: Collection, deck builder, deck slots, card detail (routes: collection, decks, deckbuilder, gallery)

**Score: 5/10** — 20 defects (5 critical)

## The single worst thing

The collection is a 8,381px-tall undifferentiated 7-column contact sheet in which roughly half the tiles are the same procedural placeholder — a floating black circle over a striped vertical gradient with "ART PENDING" stamped in the corner (scripts/screenshots/recon/collection/10-grid-scrolled.png: 20 of 21 visible cards are the black dot). The placeholder is not even drawing what it intends: placeholderArt.ts fills the torso with rgba(#05030b,0.9) against a backdrop whose lower gradient stop is palette.abyss, so 62% of the silhouette is invisible and only the head survives as a hole. Then the grid presents that placeholder at exactly the same visual weight, size and elevation as the finished painted cards, in a flat grid on a near-black void with no shelf, no lighting, no grouping and no pagination. Hearthstone never shows you a wall of identical anything — the collection is a physical shelf, paged eight cards at a time with a page-turn, so repetition is impossible; MTG Arena groups by rarity with sticky headers and a scrubber. Here the first impression of the game's card library is "a spreadsheet of unfinished assets".

## Defects

### 1. [CRITICAL] Half the collection renders the same procedural placeholder, and the placeholder is broken: the silhouette's torso is drawn at rgba(#05030b,0.9) over a backdrop that fades to palette.abyss, so the body vanishes and only the head circle reads — a black dot in the middle of every unpainted card. 121 art files exist in public/assets/art for 245 collectible cards.

- **Where:** src/ui/cardRenderer/placeholderArt.ts:99-112 (fill value), :45-50 (backdrop stops), :226-235 ("ART PENDING" watermark); visible everywhere in src/ui/screens/collectionScreen.ts:219
- **Why it fails:** AAA-BAR §1 (nothing is flat: the placeholder is a two-stop gradient plus three linear cones, no texture, no contact shadow) and §0 (a viewer instantly names the indie project). Hearthstone has no unfinished-art state at all; Gwent's unowned cards are still full paintings, greyscaled. Shipping a build with ART PENDING stamped across half the library fails the one-sentence test on its own.
- **Fix:** Two changes, both £0. (1) Repair the silhouette: light it from the same key as the frame — fill with a vertical gradient from mix(palette.lo, #000, 0.45) at the shoulders to near-black at the hem, add a 2px rim stroke at hexToRgba(palette.hi, 0.5) on the light side, and drop the head/torso down so the figure crosses the value break rather than sitting in it. (2) Raise the composition off template: derive 3–4 distinct layouts from hashString(name) (portrait / object-on-plinth / crowd silhouette / environment) instead of one figure for all 124 cards, and drop the ART PENDING watermark from the collection size class (keep it only above 300px render width where it is a dev affordance, not a shipped label).

### 2. [CRITICAL] At 1280×720 the deck builder's action row — Save Deck, Auto-Complete, Compare, Test vs AI, Clear — is entirely off-screen. Probed live: #db-actions top = 720px, .builder-side bottom = 704px, viewport height 720. There is no scrollbar on .builder-side, so the buttons are unreachable. You cannot save a deck at the minimum supported resolution.

- **Where:** src/ui/theme/screens.css:883 (.builder-side has min-height:0 and no overflow) with :903 (.deck-list { flex: 1 1 auto; min-height: 100px }); evidence scripts/screenshots/recon/collection/13-db-720-probe.png
- **Why it fails:** AAA-BAR §9: "Every screen still has to work at 1280×720 and on a phone in landscape." This is a hard constraint that overrides aesthetics, and it is broken. MTG Arena pins the deck column's Done/Save bar to the bottom of the panel as a fixed footer that never participates in the flex squeeze.
- **Fix:** Make .builder-side a three-row grid — header block (auto), scroll region (1fr, min-height:0, containing curve/identity/list), footer (auto) — and move #db-validation and #db-actions into the footer row with a top divider that fades at both ends. Give the scroll region the shared listShell fade-mask. Drop .deck-list's min-height:100px, which is what forces the overflow.

### 3. [CRITICAL] The deck list — the single most important object on the deck builder — is a 4-row peephole. A 30-card deck is ~17 unique rows; the panel shows four and a half, with the fifth sliced horizontally through the middle of its row and no scrollbar thumb, no fade, no row count, no indication that 13 more rows exist.

- **Where:** src/ui/theme/screens.css:903 (.deck-list) and src/ui/screens/deckBuilderScreen.ts:286-313; evidence scripts/screenshots/recon/collection/09-side-panel.png
- **Why it fails:** AAA-BAR §7 ("Scrollbars styled to match" — here there is effectively no scrollbar at all) and §2 (a hard clipped edge with no treatment). Hearthstone shows all 30 deck slots simultaneously and never scrolls; MTG Arena shows the whole 60-card list in a full-height column with sticky type headers. A card game where you cannot see your own deck is not finished.
- **Fix:** After the footer fix above, give the list the full remaining panel height, add sticky cost-bucket headers ("1 Hype ·  6"), a mask-image fade at both ends of the scroll region, and an always-visible 6px thumb using --chrome-mid rather than --chrome-lo so it reads against the panel. Show "17 of 17 rows" beside the 30/30 counter.

### 4. [CRITICAL] The collection has no empty state. Search for a string that matches nothing and you get a completely blank 1300×800 rectangle — no message, no illustration, no "clear filters" call to action, no explanation. The only feedback is "0 shown" in 14px grey text 800px away in the header.

- **Where:** src/ui/screens/collectionScreen.ts:207-250 (render() loops `visible` and writes nothing when it is empty); evidence scripts/screenshots/recon/collection/07-empty-state.png
- **Why it fails:** AAA-BAR §5: "Empty states are designed too. 'You own no cards' is a moment to be charming, not a blank grid." This is worse than a blank grid — it is a blank void. Hearthstone's filtered-to-nothing state shows an empty shelf with a line of flavour copy; MTG Arena shows an illustrated card-back with "No cards match these filters" and a Reset button.
- **Fix:** Build the shared emptyState() primitive and use it here: a centred plate at ~420px with a large low-opacity Current sigil, a display-type headline ("Nothing in the racks"), one line of body naming the active filters, and a primary "Clear filters" button. Reuse the same component for the Missing/Favourites tabs and the deck-slots screen.

### 5. [CRITICAL] The character gallery is a database table with initial-letter avatars. Every tile is a near-black rectangle (rgba(255,255,255,0.02)) with a 1px border, a 3px coloured left stripe, one coloured capital letter, a name and a role word. No portrait, no art, no depth. Row rhythm is ragged: within one row, tiles with two-line names push their role label 34px lower than their neighbours, so three different baselines appear across a single row.

- **Where:** src/ui/theme/screens.css:2668-2683 (.gallery-tile, .gallery-tile-initial) and src/ui/screens/galleryScreen.ts:92-98; evidence scripts/screenshots/recon/collection/18-gallery-tiles.png
- **Why it fails:** AAA-BAR §1 bans exactly this construction — a flat fill plus a 1px solid border as the only edge treatment — and §7 demands consistent rhythm. The initial-letter circle is the Gmail-contacts convention; it is an admin-dashboard pattern, not a game's character gallery. Gwent's lore browser gives every character a full-bleed painted panel; even MTG Arena's set browser uses card art crops.
- **Fix:** Rebuild the tile on the shared CardTile primitive: a 3:4 crop of the character's card art (or the repaired placeholder) full-bleed behind a bottom scrim, name in display type on the scrim, faction crest bottom-right, raised-panel edge treatment, and a fixed tile aspect so every row's baselines align. Keep the Current stripe but run it as a gradient along the bottom edge rather than a hard 3px left border.

### 6. [MAJOR] The deck slots screen is 90% empty room. At 1600×900 one deck card and one dashed "New deck" box occupy the top-left 980×270px of a 1600×900 screen; the remaining ~1.1 million pixels are flat near-black with a faint 64px grid. The add tile is `border: 1px dashed var(--glass-border); background: none` — a wireframe placeholder shipped as final art.

- **Where:** src/ui/theme/screens.css:3482-3493 (.deck-slots-body, max-width 1100 centred) and :3523-3536 (.deck-slot-empty); src/ui/screens/deckSlotsScreen.ts:68-81; evidence scripts/screenshots/recon/collection/02-decks.png and 15-decks-720.png
- **Why it fails:** AAA-BAR §2 (four resolvable depth planes — this screen has one) and §1 (dashed 1px with no fill is the most literal possible violation). Hearthstone's deck manager fills the frame with a physical shelf of nine deck spines, each a painted hero portrait; MTG Arena tiles the whole width with art-banner deck cards. An empty room reads as unfinished, not spacious.
- **Fix:** Render all 12 slots, not just the used ones — a rack of twelve, the unused ones as recessed empty sockets with an inner shadow, a faint engraved slot number and a hairline top rim, so the screen is always full and the player can see their capacity. Widen max-width to 1440px, give the body a midground rail behind the rack, and replace the dashed box with a recessed socket plus a raised + medallion.

### 7. [MAJOR] Every card tile in the collection and every gallery tile is keyboard-unreachable. .card-cell is a bare <div> with a click listener and no tabindex, no role, no keydown handler; .gallery-tile is a bare <li> with the same. There is no way to open the card detail overlay or a character page without a mouse.

- **Where:** src/ui/screens/collectionScreen.ts:217-243 (cell = createElement("div")) and src/ui/screens/galleryScreen.ts:93 / :253-259
- **Why it fails:** AAA-BAR §9: "Accessibility is not negotiable… keyboard focus is always visible." A focus ring that can never appear on the primary interactive element of a screen is not a focus ring. The deck builder already gets this right — its pool cells are real <button>s (deckBuilderScreen.ts:235) — so the codebase disagrees with itself about the same control.
- **Fix:** Make both a <button type="button"> like the builder's pool cell, with aria-label "{name}, {rarity} {current}, {owned} owned". Add roving-tabindex arrow-key navigation across the grid (Left/Right/Up/Down by column count) so 245 cards do not mean 245 tab stops, and give the focused cell the same lift the hover gets plus the shared focus ring.

### 8. [MAJOR] The card-detail panel prints raw markdown. "**Rushwind:** summon a 1/2 **Anon**. *(Rushwind — bonus if this is not the first card you played this turn.)*" is shown with the literal asterisks, while the card canvas eight inches to the left renders the same string correctly as bold and italic. The two halves of the same screen disagree about the same sentence.

- **Where:** src/ui/screens/collectionScreen.ts:426 — `<p class="cd-effect">${card.text ? esc(card.text) : …}</p>`; evidence scripts/screenshots/recon/collection/16-hidden-trace.png
- **Why it fails:** AAA-BAR §4 (type is designed) and §0. Raw markup leaking into shipped UI is the single most recognisable sign of an unfinished build; it is the kind of thing a QA pass at any studio catches on day one.
- **Fix:** The canvas renderer already parses the **/* markup — extract that parser into a shared inlineMarkup(text) that returns escaped HTML with <strong>/<em>, and call it from cd-effect, the keyword reminder list, deck-row tooltips and the gallery card strip. Never pass card.text through esc() alone.

### 9. [MAJOR] The card-detail overlay's keyboard navigation leaks listeners exponentially. openDetail() adds a window keydown listener on every call and only removes it when a key is pressed while the overlay is already hidden. Arrow-Right therefore fires every previously-registered listener, each of which calls step(1) → openDetail() → registers another. Ten presses produce hundreds of listeners and an unpredictable final card. Closing via the ✕ or the backdrop leaves the listener attached forever, and it re-arms on the next open. Separately, the backdrop click-to-close listener is registered with { once: true }, so the first click anywhere inside the overlay (a tab, a button) consumes it and clicking the backdrop no longer closes the modal.

- **Where:** src/ui/screens/collectionScreen.ts:519-530 (onKey) and :532-538 (backdrop listener with { once: true })
- **Why it fails:** AAA-BAR §9 ("Nothing regresses… pretty and broken is broken") and §5 (every state designed — "close" is a state and it stops working after one interaction). In three of six headless captures the overlay opened, was verified open (hidden=false, one child), and had closed itself before the shot 1.5s later; it is not reliably stayable-open. MTG Arena's card zoom closes on backdrop click, Esc and right-click, every time, forever.
- **Fix:** Hoist onKey and the backdrop handler out of openDetail into the screen's construction scope — register once, read the current card from a module-level `let current`, and remove both in the Screen's dispose(). Drop { once: true }. Add an explicit `closeDetail()` that hides, clears innerHTML and restores focus to the originating card cell.

### 10. [MAJOR] The card-detail overlay is a card and a paragraph floating in a void. .cd-panel has no background, no border, no padding — the stat rows, the lore and the buttons sit directly on the backdrop scrim. The Story tab leaves ~300px of empty space between the last line of prose and the action row; the Effect tab leaves ~150px. The whole stage sits inside a 1600px viewport with 210px of nothing on each side and 70px below.

- **Where:** src/ui/theme/screens.css:714-719 (.cd-panel — flex container only, zero surface) and :585-590 (.cd-stage); evidence scripts/screenshots/recon/collection/05c-detail-debug2.png and 17-detail-story.png
- **Why it fails:** AAA-BAR §1 (text over a scrim with no plate) and §4 ("Text over imagery always has a scrim, a shadow, or a plate. Never raw"). Also §2 — the backdrop-filter: blur(14px) plus rgba(3,2,8,0.9) annihilates the collection behind rather than pushing it back, so instead of four depth planes there are two: card, and void. Gwent's card inspector puts the text on a lit plate with an ornamental rule; MTG Arena keeps the collection visible and softly blurred behind the zoom so you never lose your place.
- **Fix:** Give .cd-panel the shared raised-panel material (gradient + top rim + grain + 24px drop) with sp-5 padding, and let it fill the stage height so the action row sits on its own divided footer instead of floating. Drop the backdrop scrim to rgba(3,2,8,0.62) with blur(20px) saturate(0.7) so the grid behind survives as a recognisable, receded plane. Fill the vertical slack with content that belongs there: a "seen in these decks" strip, the craft/dust economy row, and the sibling position indicator ("14 of 245").

### 11. [MAJOR] The deck-list rows signal Current with a single letter and rarity with a coloured diamond only. Currents are Cinder/Tide/Root/Gale/Pulse/Halo/Veil/Prism, and label.charAt(0) gives "P" for both Pulse and Prism — distinguishable only by colour. The ◆ glyph carries rarity with no other cue, and there is no legend anywhere on the screen.

- **Where:** src/ui/screens/deckBuilderScreen.ts:307 (`palette.label.charAt(0)`) and :309 (`<span class="deck-row-rarity" style="color:…">◆</span>`); evidence scripts/screenshots/recon/collection/09-side-panel.png
- **Why it fails:** AAA-BAR §6 and §9: "Never signal by colour alone — that rule is already in the design system and it stays." Two Currents colliding on the same letter makes the colour the only differentiator, which is precisely the banned case. MTG Arena uses distinct mana pips with different shapes; Gwent uses distinct faction sigils.
- **Fix:** Ship the shared currentAccent() token including a distinct 12px sigil glyph per Current (drawn as an inline SVG path on the shared icon grid), and render the sigil plus a two-letter code (PU / PR) rather than one letter. Replace the rarity diamond with the same rarity gem shape the card frame already draws, at 3 distinguishable silhouettes (circle/diamond/star), and add a one-line legend under the list.

### 12. [MAJOR] The hype-curve chart is crude and its own animation is dead code. Count labels are absolutely positioned at top:-2px of the full-height track rather than above the fill, so the tallest bar's "13" is drawn in --text-faint grey directly on top of a hot magenta fill and is illegible, while short bars' counts float unattached in mid-air. There is no baseline, no axis, no gridline, and no drawn target curve despite the caption saying "2 short of the target curve". The zero bucket renders as a 2px stray underline. .curve-fill declares `transition: height` but renderStats() replaces curveHost.innerHTML wholesale, so the bars never animate — they pop in at final height.

- **Where:** src/ui/theme/screens.css:895-899 (.curve-fill transition, .curve-count top:-2px) and src/ui/screens/deckBuilderScreen.ts:374-378 (innerHTML replacement)
- **Why it fails:** AAA-BAR §4 (numbers read under pressure are high-contrast and never illegible), §3 ("Nothing ever pops in" / "Anything appearing has an entrance"), §7 (a chart with no axis is not designed). MTG Arena's curve has a labelled axis, a translucent target overlay, and the bars grow when you add a card.
- **Fix:** Build the shared chartFrame() primitive: baseline rule that fades at both ends, three faint gridlines, tabular count labels rendered above the fill top (translate by the fill height) with a 1px dark plate behind them, and the target curve drawn as a dashed ghost outline over the bars. Diff the buckets in renderStats and mutate the existing .curve-fill heights instead of replacing innerHTML, so the declared transition actually runs, staggered 40ms per bucket.

### 13. [MAJOR] Deck type counts are truncated to four characters and produce wrong words: "even 1" (event), "acti 10" (action), "equi 1" (equipment), "loca 1" (location), "reac 2" (reaction). "even 1" reads as a statement about parity.

- **Where:** src/ui/screens/deckBuilderScreen.ts:341 — `type.slice(0, 4)`; evidence scripts/screenshots/recon/collection/09-side-panel.png
- **Why it fails:** AAA-BAR §4 (a real type hierarchy with designed labels) and §0. MTG Arena writes "Creatures 15 · Lands 24" in full because the deck composition summary is read at a glance, not decoded. A slice(0,4) is a placeholder someone forgot to replace.
- **Fix:** Write the plurals explicitly — Characters 15, Actions 10, Reactions 2, Equipment 1, Locations 1, Events 1 — as small-caps labels at +0.08em tracking with a tabular numeral, wrapping to two rows. If the 380px column is too narrow, drop to a single-line summary with the icon set instead of words.

### 14. [MAJOR] Filter chips, search field and the ownership segmented control are flat fills. .filter-chip is rgba(255,255,255,0.06) with `border: 1px solid transparent`; .ownership-tab.active is `background: var(--accent)` — a solid #b56cff lozenge with no gradient, no inner highlight, no shadow; the 44px search pill is a 1px rgba(180,160,255,0.16) hairline that is effectively invisible at 1x and carries no magnifier icon, no clear affordance and no inline result count. The search field is 44px tall while the segment control is 38px, so two controls in the same row disagree about height.

- **Where:** src/ui/theme/screens.css:486-500 (.filter-chip), :505-514 (.search-input), :520-525 (.ownership-tab / .active); evidence scripts/screenshots/recon/collection/19-toolbar.png at 3x
- **Why it fails:** AAA-BAR §1 bans a solid #hex on any surface larger than an icon and bans `border: 1px solid` as the only edge treatment; §5 requires rest/hover/active/focus-visible/disabled to all be designed and the active chip here changes only background-color. Hearthstone's mana filter is a physical stone tab that depresses; Arena's colour pips light and gain a rim.
- **Fix:** Route all three through the shared raised-panel material at chip scale: active chip = a vertical gradient from the chip colour at 34% to 18%, a 1px top rim at rgba(255,255,255,0.18), a 1px black bottom, an inset 0 1px 0 highlight and a 0 2px 4px contact shadow; rest chip = recessed (inset shadow, no rim). Give the segmented control a recessed track with the active pill physically raised out of it, and match both to the 44px control height. Add a 16px search glyph from the shared icon set at the field's left inset and an × clear button at the right.

### 15. [MAJOR] Card hover is a lift and nothing else. .card-cell:hover translates -8px, scales 1.03 and adds a drop shadow. No lighting change, no rim, no rarity glow, no specular sweep, no name callout, no neighbour recession, no sound hook. The lifted card's shadow lands directly on the card below with no gap. .cd-arrow:hover changes only opacity 0.55 → 1 on a transparent, borderless 46px glyph with no hit-target surface at all.

- **Where:** src/ui/theme/screens.css:541 (.card-cell:hover), :689-711 (.cd-arrow, .cd-arrow:hover); evidence scripts/screenshots/recon/collection/11-hover-forced.png
- **Why it fails:** AAA-BAR §5: "A hover that only changes opacity is not a hover. The reference games move the element, light it, sound it and often scale it — all three at once, in under 120ms." Hearthstone's collection hover lifts the card, sweeps a glint across the frame and plays a paper rustle on the same frame. §3's "secondary motion" is completely absent here.
- **Fix:** Compose the hover from the shared primitives: translateY(-10px) + scale(1.04) on --ease-overshoot at 110ms, a rarity-glow box-shadow ramped in over the same window, a 400ms specular sweep via a masked linear-gradient pseudo-element, the two horizontal neighbours nudged 3px outward and dimmed to 0.85 filter brightness, and sfx.ui.hover fired on pointerenter so sound and visual land on the same frame. Give .cd-arrow a real 46px circular surface (recessed track, raised chevron) and move it 6px on hover.

### 16. [MAJOR] Adding or removing a card rebuilds the entire deck list and the entire pool from scratch. renderList() sets listHost.innerHTML = "" and recreates every row; renderPool() sets poolHost.innerHTML = "" and re-renders 100+ canvases at 150px each. Nothing flies from the pool card to the list, no row highlights, no count ticks, the whole panel snaps to a new state.

- **Where:** src/ui/screens/deckBuilderScreen.ts:229-283 (renderPool) and :286-313 (renderList)
- **Why it fails:** AAA-BAR §3 ("Nothing snaps… Everything overlaps… stagger by 30–60ms") and §7 ("Nothing ever pops in. Anything appearing has an entrance"). Also a §9 performance risk: 100+ canvas re-rasterisations on every click will not hold 30fps on the low tier. Hearthstone flies the card into the deck list and flashes the row gold; MTG Arena slides the row in and rolls the count digit.
- **Fix:** Key both renders by card id and reconcile — update badges and counts in place, only create/destroy the rows that actually changed. Add an insert animation: the new row enters at scaleY(0.6)/opacity 0 on --ease-arrive over 240ms while the rows below stagger down 40ms apart, and the pool cell that was clicked plays a 160ms press-and-return with a ghost card tweening to the list position.

### 17. [MAJOR] There is no drag-and-drop anywhere in the deck builder. Grepping src/ui/screens for pointerdown / dragstart / draggable / setDragImage returns exactly one hit, in battleScreen.ts. Cards are add-on-click only; there is no drag ghost, no drop-zone highlight, no snap-back.

- **Where:** src/ui/screens/deckBuilderScreen.ts (whole file — cell.addEventListener("click", …) at :251 is the only add path)
- **Why it fails:** The brief judges drag feedback, and AAA-BAR §5 requires every interaction state to be designed. MTG Arena lets you drag a card from the pool into the deck column with a tilted ghost following the cursor and the column edge lighting up; Hearthstone lets you drag a deck-list row out to remove it. Click-only is functional and reads as a prototype.
- **Fix:** Build the shared dragSurface() helper (pointerdown/move/up, no HTML5 DnD, no new dependency) and wire it to both directions: pool → list adds, list → outside removes. Ghost = the card canvas at 0.9 scale with a 10° tilt and a 40px soft shadow, following the pointer with a 60ms lag; the deck panel gains a rim glow and its rows part to open the insertion gap; releasing outside a target springs the ghost back to origin on an overshoot ease. Keep click-to-add as the accessible path and never make drag the only route.

### 18. [MINOR] Unicode and emoji stand in for icons throughout the domain: 🔒/🔓 (full-colour OS emoji) for lock, ✦ for shards, ★/☆ for favourite, ⌖ for build-around, ▤ for deck slots, ✕ for close, ‹ › for the detail arrows, ◆ for rarity, ✓ for legal. They come from different type families at different optical sizes and stroke weights, and the emoji render in the host OS's own art style — a glossy Segoe padlock in a neon nightlife UI.

- **Where:** src/ui/screens/collectionScreen.ts:235 and :502 (🔒), :229 (★), :304 (✕), :353 (‹ ›), :469 (✦); src/ui/screens/deckBuilderScreen.ts:267 (⌖), :309 (◆), :387 (✓); src/ui/screens/deckSlotsScreen.ts:49 (▤)
- **Why it fails:** AAA-BAR §7: "Icons on a single grid, single stroke weight, single optical size." A colour emoji is the loudest possible violation — it is somebody else's art direction rendered at full saturation inside your palette, and it changes shape between Windows, macOS and Android.
- **Fix:** Draw a 12-glyph inline-SVG set on a 24px grid at 1.5px stroke (search, close, chevron-left/right, plus, lock, lock-open, star, star-filled, crosshair, deck, shard) as a shared iconSet module exporting path data, and replace every glyph above. Zero cost, zero dependencies, and it fixes the same problem on every other screen at once.

### 19. [MINOR] Crafting is not capped at the copy limit. Add sticky cost-bucket headers to the card grid ("0 HYPE · 12 cards") plus a right-edge cost scrubber, or page it eight rows at a time with a page-turn.

## Plan

1. FOUNDATION FIRST — do not start any screen work until these land, or fifteen builders will each invent a different bevel.
